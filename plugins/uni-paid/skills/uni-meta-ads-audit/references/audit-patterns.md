# Patterns from past UNI Meta audits

Anonymised. Use these as hypotheses to test in a new account, never as conclusions. Each pattern names the check that confirms or kills it.

| # | Pattern | Seen in | Confirm with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tracking is fine; the problem is media cost or reach.** Purchase EMQ 9+ with CAPI live, and the drop is in CPM, not site conversion | 4 of 4 audits | Section 1 PASS + LPV-to-purchase flat across blocks |
| 2 | **Spend up, reach flat.** Best ROAS block was the lowest-spend, smallest-reach block (warm buyers). Doubling spend added frequency, not people | Kidswear / dancewear | Weekly reach vs spend; age band frequency |
| 3 | **CTR flat, CPC up = auction, not creative.** Do not prescribe new creative for a CPM problem alone | Dancewear, jewelry | First vs last week table |
| 4 | **One old static or one video carries 40 to 80% of spend.** Nothing newer ever earned budget because it sits in the same ad set as the incumbent | Jewelry, audio | Concept rollup + launch dates + ad set membership |
| 5 | **Videos hold attention but do not sell in the first 2 seconds.** Avg watch 2 to 4 seconds, link CTR below statics | Dancewear, audio | p25 / impressions, avg watch time, link CTR by format |
| 6 | **Reels and Stories bought with feed statics.** Or the reverse: Reels excluded by manual placements while the account is video-led | Jewelry, audio | Placement table + ad set placement settings |
| 7 | **One campaign per promo or per interest.** Every new idea restarts learning; no ad set ever reaches 50 events a week | All four | Spending ad sets vs purchases per week; learning status |
| 8 | **Interest "tests" that are not tests.** Single interest, Advantage+ audience off or on without a broad control, under-funded for a week | Jewelry, audio | Ad set targeting + budget vs 1x CPA per week |
| 9 | **Winners paused with their campaign.** Best ads never moved to an always-on slot | Dancewear | Paused campaigns with ROAS above account average |
| 10 | **Cart or checkout is the leak.** ATC fine, IC-to-purchase or ATC-to-IC far below peers. Common causes: shipping or duties first seen at checkout, threshold inconsistency, missing express pay | Menswear, jewelry, audio | Peer percentiles on checkout steps + live product page and review text |
| 11 | **ATC inflated by a cart app.** Gift / upsell apps fire extra AddToCart; ATC rate jumps while ATC-to-purchase halves | Jewelry, dancewear | Test Events while adding one item |
| 12 | **Mixed attribution settings across campaigns.** One campaign counts 1-day view and engage-through, the rest click only | Dancewear, audio | `attribution_setting` per campaign |
| 13 | **Stale customer list.** Lookalikes seeded from a list 6 to 9 months old | Dancewear, jewelry | Custom audience update date |
| 14 | **AOV just under the free-shipping or gift line.** The offer math caps the result more than the ads do | Dancewear, jewelry | AOV vs threshold |

Add a row when a new audit confirms a pattern twice. Remove a row that has been wrong twice.
